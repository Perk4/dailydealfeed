# DailyDealFeed Strategy

**Last Updated:** 2026-02-21

---

## Decisions Made

### Monetization
- **Pure affiliate** (Amazon Associates) — no sponsored products for now

### Posting
- **3x/day** on both IG and TikTok
- **Manual posting** until pipeline is consistent
- **Single handle** (@dailydealfeed) on both platforms

### Content
- **Product videos only** — no meme/trending content mixing yet
- Focus on quality before expanding strategy

### Voice/TTS
- **Skip ElevenLabs** for now — use current TTS solution

### A/B Testing
- **Skip** — not convinced it helps at this stage
- Instead: **benchmark against @codesinred** (the gold standard)

### Scaling
Scale ONLY when:
1. ✅ Pipeline consistently identifies good products
2. ✅ Videos are consistently high quality
3. ✅ Link site updates correctly

**All three components need massive improvements first.**
