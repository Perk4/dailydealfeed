## 🧠 Hook Psychology (What Actually Works)

A killer hook combines **4 elements** in the first 1-3 seconds:

| Element | What it does | Example |
|---------|--------------|---------|
| **Pattern Interrupt** | Breaks the scroll — something unexpected | Starting mid-sentence, unusual claim, visual mismatch |
| **Curiosity Gap** | Creates NEED to know more | "My dermatologist is gonna hate me for this..." |
| **Emotional Trigger** | Visceral response (shock, humor, FOMO, disgust) | "What came out of my couch... I might throw up" |
| **Visual-Audio Sync** | Clip and words hit at the EXACT same moment | Shocked face + "I didn't expect THIS" |

## 🔥 THE 10 HOOK FORMULAS THAT WORK

### 1. **THE FORBIDDEN SECRET**
> "My [AUTHORITY] is gonna hate me for sharing this..."

- Creates: Curiosity + slight controversy + authority
- Best for: Skincare, wellness, anything with expert endorsement
- Clip vibe: **reveal** or **reaction**
- Example: *"My dermatologist is gonna hate me for this $19 secret..."*

### 2. **THE DISGUSTING REVEAL**
> "What came out of my [THING]... I'm [EMOTION]"

- Creates: Morbid curiosity + disgust (most powerful emotion for engagement)
- Best for: Cleaning products, carpet cleaners, anything that shows gunk
- Clip vibe: **shocked** (reaction shot of disgust)
- Example: *"What came out of my 'clean' couch... I might throw up"*

### 3. **THE PRICE SHOCK**
> "This $[PRICE] [THING] did what my $[BIG PRICE] couldn't"

- Creates: Disbelief + value proposition + immediate interest
- Best for: Any affordable product
- Clip vibe: **shocked** or **reveal**
- Example: *"This $5 paste did what my $200 cleaning service couldn't"*

### 4. **THE BEFORE/AFTER TEASE**
> "POV: before and after [THING]... I wasn't ready"

- Creates: Anticipation of transformation
- Best for: Skincare, cleaning, any visible results product
- Clip vibe: **reveal** (transformation)
- Example: *"POV: my skin before and after this $19 cream... I wasn't ready"*

### 5. **THE ACCIDENTAL DISCOVERY**
> "I found this [THING] by accident and now I'm [EMOTION]"

- Creates: Relatability + curiosity about what changed
- Best for: Any product that feels like a hidden gem
- Clip vibe: **reaction** (pleasant surprise)
- Example: *"I bought this by accident and now I'm genuinely obsessed"*

### 6. **THE BETRAYAL**
> "Nobody told me [THING] and I'm [EMOTION]"

- Creates: FOMO + slight outrage + need to know
- Best for: Products everyone else seems to know about
- Clip vibe: **fail** → **reveal**
- Example: *"Nobody told me about this and I'm actually mad"*

### 7. **THE MID-STORY START**
> "...so anyway that's why I own 7 of these now"

- Creates: Pattern interrupt + curiosity (wait, what happened?)
- Best for: Any product you could plausibly buy multiples of
- Clip vibe: **reaction** (casual excitement)
- Example: *"...anyway that's why I own 4 of these now"*

### 8. **THE SKEPTIC CONVERTED**
> "I called this a scam for [TIME]... until [EVENT]"

- Creates: Trust + transformation arc in seconds
- Best for: Products that seem too good to be true (Pink Stuff, gadgets)
- Clip vibe: **shocked** → **reveal**
- Example: *"I called this a scam for 2 years... then I tried it on my stove"*

### 9. **THE LATE NIGHT VIBE**
> "It's [TIME] and I [EMOTION] about [THING]"

- Creates: Relatability + intimacy + emotional connection
- Best for: Cozy/aesthetic products, nighttime routines
- Clip vibe: **cozy** (candles, dim lighting)
- Example: *"It's 3am and I'm genuinely emotional about this lamp"*

### 10. **THE CHALLENGE ACCEPTED**
> "You said [PRODUCT] couldn't [IMPOSSIBLE THING]. Watch."

- Creates: Anticipation + proof-is-coming energy
- Best for: Cleaning, tech, anything demonstrable
- Clip vibe: **reveal** (transformation in progress)
- Example: *"You said this paste can't fix burnt pans. Watch this."*

## ❌ WHAT NOT TO DO (Common Mistakes)

### 1. **Starting with the product name**
- ❌ "CeraVe Moisturizing Cream is amazing..."
- ✅ "My dermatologist is gonna hate me for this..."

Product name = scroll. Mystery = stop.

### 2. **Being too polished/branded**
- ❌ "Hey guys! Today I want to talk about..."
- ✅ "...anyway that's why I own 7 of these"

Corporate energy = skip. Raw energy = watch.

### 3. **Burying the emotion**
- ❌ "This cleaner works pretty well actually"
- ✅ "What came out of my couch... I might throw up"

Mild = forgettable. Extreme = memorable.

### 4. **Mismatched clip-to-hook**
- ❌ Saying "I'm disgusted" while showing happy reaction clip
- ✅ Audio emotion matches visual emotion EXACTLY

Mismatch = confusion. Sync = impact.

### 5. **Giving away the answer**
- ❌ "This is the best moisturizer and here's why"
- ✅ "My skin did something weird after 3 days..."

Answer = no reason to watch. Mystery = must watch.

### 6. **Starting slow**
- ❌ [3 seconds of nothing] then hook
- ✅ Hook hits at frame 1, audio hits at 0.5 seconds

TikTok algorithm measures 1-second retention. Hit IMMEDIATELY.

### 7. **Using weak emotional words**
- ❌ "I like this" / "It's nice" / "Pretty good"
- ✅ "I'm obsessed" / "I'm disturbed" / "I'm genuinely mad"

Weak = generic. Extreme = engaging.

> "WAIT. Watch what comes out of this 'clean' carpet..."
- **Pattern interrupt:** "WAIT" — stops scroll

- **Visual:** Carpet being cleaned, dirty water visible

### Example 2: Skincare Content (15M views pattern)
> "My esthetician told me to stop... but look at my skin now"
- **Pattern interrupt:** Authority said stop (controversy)
- **Curiosity gap:** What happened?
- **Emotional trigger:** Rebellion satisfaction
- **Visual:** Before/after skin transformation

### Example 3: Kitchen Gadget (8M views pattern)
> "I bought this as a joke and now it's 6am and I can't stop"
- **Pattern interrupt:** "As a joke" (low expectations)
- **Curiosity gap:** Why can't they stop?
- **Emotional trigger:** Relatability (impulse buying)
- **Visual:** Using product at weird hour

> "If you know, you know... if you don't, your life is about to change"
- **Pattern interrupt:** Inside joke energy
- **Curiosity gap:** What's the secret?
- **Emotional trigger:** FOMO (being left out)
- **Visual:** Product reveal with dreamy lighting

## 🔄 A/B TESTING HOOKS

When testing hooks, vary ONE element:
1. **Test emotion:** "I'm disturbed" vs "I'm obsessed" vs "I'm mad"
2. **Test authority:** "Dermatologist" vs "My mom" vs no authority
3. **Test price position:** Lead with price vs hide price
4. **Test clip vibe:** Same hook, different clip energy

Track: Watch time %, swipe-away rate, comments about the hook
