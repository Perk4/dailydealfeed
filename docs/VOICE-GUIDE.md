# Voice Guide - DailyDealFeed

> **Last Updated:** 2026-02-20
> **Purpose:** Defining the authentic, relatable voice for all DailyDealFeed content

---

## The Core Vibe

**You're a friend sharing a find, not a salesperson pushing a product.**

Imagine you're texting your group chat about something you bought that actually works. That's the energy. Casual, genuine, maybe a little excited, but never trying too hard.

---

## Tone Spectrum

```
❌ Infomercial -------- You Are Here -------- Friend texting ✅
   "BUY NOW!"           ↓                    "okay you need this"
                    Chill, honest,
                    low-key excited
```

---

## Voice Characteristics

### ✅ DO: Sound Like This

| Quality | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **Casual** | "okay so" / "honestly" / "like" / "literally" |
| **Genuine** | "I wasn't sure at first but..." / "I was skeptical" |
| **Relatable** | "you know when..." / "we've all been there" |
| **Low-key excited** | "I'm kinda obsessed" / "it's so good" |
| **Conversational** | "grab it if you want" / "link's up there" |
| **Honest** | "it's not perfect but..." / "for the price, it's great" |

### ❌ DON'T: Sound Like This

| Anti-Pattern | Why It's Bad |
|--------------|--------------|
| "AMAZING deal!" | Feels like an ad, not a rec |
| "Must-have!" | Pushy, creates pressure |
| "You NEED this!" | Demanding, salesy |
| "Limited time only!" | Artificial urgency |
| "Best product ever!" | Hyperbolic, loses trust |
| "Link in bio NOW!" | Aggressive CTA |
| "Don't miss out!" | Fear-based marketing |

## Language Patterns

### Natural Transitions
- "so" / "okay so" / "anyway"
- "honestly" / "literally" / "like"
- "but here's the thing"
- "and the best part is"

### Price Integration
✅ "twenty-two bucks" / "under twenty"
✅ "it's like seventeen dollars"
✅ "only nine bucks which is insane"
❌ "$21.99" (spoken as "twenty-one ninety-nine" sounds robotic)

### Product References
✅ Use nicknames: "snail mucin" not "COSRX Snail Mucin 96% Power Repairing Essence 3.38 fl.oz"
✅ One clear mention is enough
❌ Don't read the full Amazon title
❌ Don't repeat the same name multiple times

### Benefit Language
✅ "my skin has never looked better"
✅ "I actually look forward to cleaning now"
✅ "game changer for my morning routine"
❌ "optimizes hydration levels" (too clinical)
❌ "revolutionary formula" (too marketing)

## Authenticity Signals

Things that make content feel real:

### Personal Touches
- "I've been using this for like a month"
- "my roommate got me hooked on this"
- "bought it at 2am, no regrets"
- "I didn't believe the hype until..."

### Casual Imperfection
- "it's not like, life-changing, but it's solid"
- "okay the packaging is kinda ugly but it works"
- "I was skeptical at first"

### Specific Details
- "I use this every morning"
- "my second bottle already"
- "use it after the shower"

### Honest Reactions
- "I'm kinda obsessed not gonna lie"
- "lowkey the best purchase I've made"
- "I didn't expect to like it this much"

## Energy Calibration

### Match Energy to Product

| Product Type | Energy Level | Example Tone |
|--------------|--------------|--------------|
| Skincare/Beauty | Warm, enthusiastic | "okay so my skin has literally never been smoother" |
| Kitchen/Cleaning | Satisfied, practical | "finally something that actually works" |
| Comfort items | Cozy, content | "like walking on literal clouds" |
| Tech/Gadgets | Impressed, practical | "this thing is actually legit" |
| Fun/Quirky items | Playful, amused | "bought this as a joke and now I use it daily" |

### Avoid:
- Over-excitement for boring products
- Flat energy for actually cool stuff
- Same energy for everything (variety matters)

## CTA Philosophy

**The goal:** Mention the link without making it feel like a sales pitch.

### Soft CTAs (Use These)
- "link in bio"
- "[price], link's in bio"
- "if you want it, link's up there"
- "go grab it"

### Medium CTAs (Occasional)
- "trust me on this one, link in bio"
- "you won't regret it"

### Avoid (Never)
- "CLICK NOW"
- "BUY IMMEDIATELY"
- "Don't wait!"
- "Limited stock!"

## Script Flow Template

```
[HOOK - 2-3 seconds]
{Curiosity-sparking opener}

[BODY - 4-7 seconds]  
{Benefit or brief context} + {Why it matters} + {Price naturally integrated}

[CTA - 2-3 seconds]
{Soft close}
```

### Example Flow:
> "Why did no one tell me about this face mist? [HOOK]
> It's basically a spa reset for your face. Rose water, super refreshing, and it's like nine bucks. [BODY]
> Link in bio if you want it." [CTA]

## Common Mistakes to Fix

### ❌ Current Pattern (Bad)
> "Check this out: COSRX Snail Mucin 96% Power Repairing Essence... COSRX Snail Mucin 96% Power Repairing Essence. Only $21.99... Link in bio."

**Problems:**
- Generic hook ("check this out")
- Full product name repeated twice
- No benefit mentioned
- Robotic flow

### ✅ Improved Version (Good)
> "Okay but why is everyone sleeping on snail mucin? My skin hasn't looked this good in years. Twenty-two bucks. Link in bio."

**Why it works:**
- Curiosity hook
- Natural product reference
- Clear benefit
- Conversational price
- Soft CTA

---

## Voice Checklist

Before publishing any script, verify:

- [ ] Would I actually say this to a friend?
- [ ] Does it sound like a person or a commercial?
- [ ] Is there at least one real benefit mentioned?
- [ ] Is the product name natural (not Amazon title)?
- [ ] Does the price sound spoken, not read?
- [ ] Is the CTA soft and non-pushy?
- [ ] Does the energy match the product?

---

## TL;DR

1. **Be a friend, not a salesperson**
2. **Lead with benefits, not features**
3. **Keep it casual and conversational**
4. **One product mention is enough**
5. **Soft CTAs always**
6. **Read it out loud - does it sound human?**
