# Script Templates - DailyDealFeed

> **Last Updated:** 2026-02-20
> **Purpose:** Standardized hook templates for engaging, authentic short-form video content

## Current State Analysis

Reviewed 21+ approved videos. Current scripts are repetitive and low-engagement:
- All hooks use: "Check this out: [product name]"
- Voiceovers repeat product name twice
- No emotional connection or storytelling

**Opportunity:** Implement varied hook styles to increase engagement and feel more authentic.

---

## Hook Templates (8 Styles)

### 1. 🔍 Discovery Hook
*"Just found this and had to share..."*

Best for: Trending products, things that feel like finds

**Templates:**
- "Okay so I just found this and I'm obsessed"
- "Why did no one tell me about [product]?"
- "I found this on Amazon at 2am and now I can't stop using it"
- "Where has this been all my life?"

**Example:**
> "Why did no one tell me about snail mucin? Like... it's $22 and my skin has never looked better."

---

### 2. ❓ Question Hook
*Spark curiosity immediately*

Best for: Problem-solving products, beauty/skincare

**Templates:**
- "You know that feeling when [relatable problem]?"
- "Ever wonder why everyone's obsessed with [product]?"
- "What if I told you [benefit] for under $[price]?"
- "You've probably seen this everywhere but does it actually work?"

**Example:**
> "You know that feeling when your skin looks tired even when you're not? This $17 sunscreen fixed that for me."

---

### 3. 😫 Problem Hook
*Lead with pain point*

Best for: Solutions, cleaning products, organization

**Templates:**
- "I used to hate [task] until I found this"
- "If you're still using [old solution], let me change your life"
- "Okay so [problem] was ruining my [thing]..."
- "I was today years old when I learned [insight]"

**Example:**
> "I used to hate cleaning my pans until I found Bar Keeper's Friend. Nine bucks. Looks brand new now."

---

### 4. 🎯 Direct Flex Hook
*Confident, straight to the point*

Best for: Tech gadgets, home items, statement products

**Templates:**
- "This $[price] [product] does what $[higher price] ones can't"
- "One thing I'll never gatekeep:"
- "If you only buy one thing this month, make it this"
- "This is going viral for a reason"

**Example:**
> "One thing I'll never gatekeep: this $25 white noise machine. Best sleep I've had in years."

---

### 5. 📖 Mini Story Hook
*Start with a tiny narrative*

Best for: Lifestyle products, anything with a personal connection

**Templates:**
- "So my friend kept telling me about [product]..."
- "I bought this as a joke and now I use it every day"
- "Three months with this [product] and honestly..."
- "My [person] got me this and now I understand the hype"

**Example:**
> "I bought these cloud slides as a joke. Three weeks later I own four pairs. They're $17."

---

### 6. ⚡ Social Proof Hook
*Lean into what others are saying*

Best for: Trending items, TikTok famous products

**Templates:**
- "Everyone's been asking about [product]"
- "You've probably seen this all over your FYP"
- "Okay TikTok made me buy this and..."
- "This has like 50k reviews for a reason"

**Example:**
> "TikTok made me buy this lip sleeping mask and honestly? It deserves the hype. $24."

---

### 7. 💰 Value Hook
*Lead with the deal*

Best for: Budget-friendly items, dupes, surprising prices

**Templates:**
- "Under $[price] and it [impressive benefit]"
- "This is basically [expensive thing] for [fraction]"
- "I can't believe this is only $[price]"
- "$[price] and it outperforms [expensive brand]"

**Example:**
> "Under twenty bucks and this face mist is basically a spa in a bottle. Rose water hits different."

---

### 8. 🚀 Urgency Hook (Use Sparingly)
*Create gentle FOMO without being pushy*

Best for: Limited deals, popular items that sell out

**Templates:**
- "This keeps selling out so grab it while you can"
- "Not sure how long this deal's gonna last"
- "If this is still in stock, you're lucky"

**Example:**
> "This Stanley tumbler keeps selling out. If it's in stock, don't sleep on it."

## Script Structure

### Timing Breakdown (10-15 seconds)
| Section | Duration | Purpose |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Hook | 2-3s | Grab attention, spark curiosity |
| Body | 4-7s | Key benefit, brief context |
| CTA | 2-3s | Soft close, link reference |

### Body Structure Options

**Benefit-First:**
> "[Hook]... [Key benefit]. [Supporting detail]. [Price]."

**Story-Benefit:**
> "[Hook]... [Brief context of use]. [Result/benefit]. [Price]."

**Feature-Benefit:**
> "[Hook]... [Standout feature]. [Why it matters]. [Price]."

---

## CTA Variations

**Soft (Preferred):**
- "Link's in my bio"
- "[Price], link in bio"
- "Go grab it, link's up there"

**Medium:**
- "If you want it, link's in bio"
- "Trust me on this one, link in bio"

**Avoid (Too Pushy):**
- ❌ "BUY THIS NOW"
- ❌ "You NEED this"
- ❌ "Click the link immediately"

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing any script:

- [ ] Hook creates curiosity in first 2 seconds
- [ ] Doesn't repeat product name unnecessarily
- [ ] Includes at least one benefit (not just features)
- [ ] Price feels natural, not forced
- [ ] CTA is soft and conversational
- [ ] Sounds like something you'd actually say to a friend
- [ ] Under 15 seconds when read aloud

---

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

❌ "Check this out: [full product name]" (boring, repetitive)
❌ Repeating the exact same product name twice
❌ Reading Amazon title verbatim
❌ No emotional hook or benefit
❌ "AMAZING DEAL MUST HAVE"
❌ Robotic listing of features
❌ Aggressive CTAs

---

## Implementation Notes

When generating scripts:
1. Select hook style based on product category
2. Extract 1-2 key benefits from product data
3. Keep product mentions natural (nickname okay)
4. Round prices for easier speaking ($21.99 → "twenty-two bucks")
5. Test by reading aloud - does it sound human?
